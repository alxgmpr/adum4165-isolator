# Würth Elektronik 750313638 — WE-PPTI Push-Pull Transformer — Pin Table

Source: `datasheets/750313638.pdf` (Würth Elektronik Midcom, order code 750313638,
revision 009.002, dated 2024-01-11), pages 1-2 (Dimensions/Schematic/Electrical
Properties table, and Typical Application tables).

Package: 6-pin SMD, 12.7 mm x 9.14 mm x 7.62 mm max (per Dimensions drawing).

## Pin-number -> name -> function

| Pin # | Winding | Role | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | N1 (primary, half 1) | Primary terminal | One end of primary winding half N1 (opposite end meets center tap at pin 2). Push-pull drive terminal A. |
| 2 | N1/N2 junction | **Primary center tap** | Center tap of primary winding (N1+N2 in series between pins 1 and 3). Connects to VIN in a push-pull driver topology (e.g. SN6505B center-tap feed). |
| 3 | N2 (primary, half 2) | Primary terminal | One end of primary winding half N2 (opposite end meets center tap at pin 2). Push-pull drive terminal B. |
| 4 | N4 (secondary, half 2) | Secondary terminal | One end of secondary winding half N4 (opposite end meets center tap at pin 5). |
| 5 | N3/N4 junction | **Secondary center tap** | Center tap of secondary winding (N3+N4 in series between pins 6 and 4). Available for a center-tapped full-wave rectifier topology on the secondary, or can be left unconnected if a full-bridge rectifier across pins 6/4 is used instead. |
| 6 | N3 (secondary, half 1) | Secondary terminal | One end of secondary winding half N3 (opposite end meets center tap at pin 5). |

Pin-1 identification: marked with a dot on the package top (per "Product Marking"
table, "Pin 1" = dot + lot/date code).

## Winding configuration (for Task 5 rectifier topology)

- **Primary**: two series halves N1 (pins 1-2) and N2 (pins 2-3), **with center
  tap at pin 2**. Total primary = N1+N2, measured pins 1-3.
- **Secondary**: two series halves N3 (pins 6-5) and N4 (pins 5-4), **with center
  tap at pin 5**. Total secondary = N3+N4, measured pins 6-4.
- Both windings are center-tapped (symmetric push-pull transformer, "WE-PPTI"
  = Würth Push-Pull Transformer Isolated series) — this is a true 3-terminal
  primary / 3-terminal secondary part, not a simple 2-winding transformer.
- **Rectifier topology implication for Task 5**: because the secondary has a
  center tap (pin 5), a full-wave center-tapped rectifier (2 diodes, one each
  from pins 6 and 4 to the output, with pin 5 as the return/ground reference)
  is directly supported without needing a 4-diode bridge. Alternatively, pins
  6 and 4 can be used as a plain 2-terminal secondary (ignoring the center tap,
  or tying it off) and rectified with a full bridge. Given TI's typical
  application circuits for SN6505B-driven push-pull isolated supplies normally
  pair this transformer's center-tapped secondary with a 2-diode center-tapped
  rectifier, that is the expected topology — confirm against the SN6505B
  datasheet's reference design before finalizing Task 5.

## Turns ratio

**n = (N1+N2) : (N3+N4) = 1 : 1.3** (per Electrical Properties table).

## Isolation rating

- **Insulation Test Voltage (V_T)**: **5000 V RMS**, tested primary (pins 1,2)
  to secondary (pins 3,4) [table header: "N1,2 => N3,4"].
- **Interwinding capacitance (C_WW)**: 4.75 pF typ (N1,2 <-> N3,4 @ 100 kHz / 10 mV).
- **Certified/designed-to-comply working-voltage ratings** (from "Designed to
  Comply With" / "Complies With" tables on datasheet page 2):
  - IEC60664-1: reinforced insulation, working voltage 490 Vrms / 700 Vpk, up to
    2 km altitude.
  - IEC62368-1 / EN62368-1 / UL62368-1 / CSA62368-1 / AS-NZS62368.1 / J62368-1:
    reinforced insulation, working voltage 800 Vrms / 1400 Vpk, Overvoltage
    Category II up to 4 km altitude (or Cat III up to 3 km altitude).
  - IEC61800-5-1: 9.2 mm min creepage, 8.0 mm min clearance between primary
    and secondary.
  - IEC60950-1 / EN60950-1 / UL60950-1 / CSA60950-1 / AS-NZS60950.1: reinforced
    insulation, working voltage 250 Vrms, Overvoltage Category II, Pollution
    Degree 2, up to 2 km altitude.
  - IEC60601-1(ed); am1 (medical): two means of patient protection (2 MOPP) up
    to working voltage of 250 Vrms.
  - Also (separate "Complies With" block on page 1): IEC62368-1/EN62368-1/
    UL62368-1/CSA62368-1/AS-NZS60950.1/J62368-1 reinforced insulation for a
    working voltage of 400 Vrms / 750 Vpk, Overvoltage Category II, up to 3 km
    altitude.

Typical application (per datasheet, input N1/N2, output N3/N4): Vin = 5 VDC,
Vout1 = 5 VDC (or 3.3 VDC in the reverse-direction typical-application table),
Iout1 = 0.65 A, switching frequency 300-620 kHz — consistent with an SN6505B
push-pull driver feeding this transformer's primary.
